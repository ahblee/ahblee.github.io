Photocrumb

Making new memories, season after season.

Photocrumb is a self-serve photo booth studio focused on playful, customizable photo experiences. Since 2025, I’ve worked across UI and visual design, creating seasonal photo frames, stickers, and in-booth interface elements used by customers in-store. Because the experience changes throughout the year, much of my work is about finding new ways to make each season feel fresh while still recognizably Photocrumb, turning a quick photo booth visit into something personal and worth keeping.

La Bâtie

Bringing audiences closer to the artists behind the stage.

La Bâtie is an 18-day multidisciplinary arts festival bringing together artists and audiences across Geneva. As lead designer on a five-person team, I helped shape the project from early visual explorations into a cohesive identity and digital experience, bringing multiple art directions together around the idea of revealing the deeper, often unseen sides of each artist. I also introduced a content strategy centered on artist biography, creative process, and personal picks, extending the festival beyond the stage and giving audiences more ways to discover and connect with the people behind the work.

Our asset mockups were recognized by our professor as the strongest across previous years of the course.
