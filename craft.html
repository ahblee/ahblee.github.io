Photocrumb

Making new memories, season after season.

Photocrumb is a self-serve photo booth studio focused on playful, customizable photo experiences. Since 2025, I’ve worked across UI and visual design, creating seasonal frames, stickers, and in-booth interface elements. I also designed a new mini frame format for a more portable keepsake, planned for release. Across each design, my goal is to keep the experience feeling fresh while still feeling like Photocrumb.

One of my designs generated the most revenue among Photocrumb’s frame options that day, with 1,000+ uses.

La Bâtie

Bringing audiences closer to the artists behind the stage.

La Bâtie is an 18-day multidisciplinary arts festival in Geneva. I took on a leading role in shaping the project’s visual direction, guiding imagery choices and helping define the rationale behind the overall art direction. I also developed the microsite content strategy around artist biography, creative process, and personal picks, giving audiences more ways to connect with the artists beyond a single performance.

Our asset mockups were recognized as the strongest produced in the course across previous years.

Youth KOSTA

Bringing visual touchpoints to life on-site.

KOSTA is a three-day Christian youth event bringing together attendees, speakers, and volunteers from across the globe and community. As a designer, I created nine wayfinding poster sets in both horizontal and vertical formats for speakers and key service areas. I also led the setup of the merch booth, photo booth, and a mirror installation used as a selfie zone. During the event, I took the lead on photo booth operations, handling media adjustments, photo printing, payments, and day-of coordination to keep everything running smoothly.
