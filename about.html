Hello,
I'm a Korean-Canadian Designer in Van me & my supporter 🐯

Ever since I was six, I’ve been around computers. With two older sisters and even my own MacBook through a school program, clicking around and figuring things out came pretty naturally :>

I started my journey in Computing Science, but quickly realized the part I loved most was actually building things. That eventually pulled me toward design, so I steered the wheel (although I still don’t have my L) toward Interactive Arts + Technology.

Since then, I’ve spent a lot of time exploring, building, and poking around systems to understand how things work. These days, AI is somewhere in the process too, but it’s still my curiosity, judgment, and slightly-too-obsessed brain behind it all.

At the end of the day, I just like making thoughtful things for people, and hopefully adding a little joy along the way :-)
