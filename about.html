About Angela

Hello, I’m a Korean-Canadian designer based in Vancouver.

My path actually started in Computing Science. I’d been glued to computers since I was five, so naturally I thought I could become all CSI with my computer skills :> Turns out, CS wasn’t quite for me, but the rare assignments where I got to build something brought back a spark I hadn’t felt in a long time.

So I steered the wheel (although I still don’t have my L) toward Interactive Arts + Technology and jumped into design. Now, in the era of AI, I love using it to explore, build, and understand systems more deeply — while keeping the human judgment, curiosity, and slightly-too-obsessed brain behind the work.

At the end of the day, I just like making meaningful things for people, and hopefully adding a little joy along the way :-)
