My path actually started in Computing Science. I’d been glued to computers since I was five, so naturally, I thought I could become all CSI with my computer skills :> Turns out, CS wasn’t quite for me. But the few assignments where I got to actually build something brought back a spark I hadn’t felt in a long time.

So I steered the wheel (although I still don’t have my L) toward Interactive Arts + Technology and somehow ended up in design. Now I spend a lot of time exploring, building, and poking around systems to understand how things work (lately with AI somewhere in the process) while keeping the judgment, curiosity, and slightly-too-obsessed brain behind it all.

At the end of the day, I just like making thoughtful things for people — and hopefully adding a little joy along the way :-)
